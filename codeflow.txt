

dataset
 - The dataset includes 30 subjects of 15 different classes. 9 out of these subjects are live subjects (dogs and cats) and 21 are objects.
 
 
 
Diffusers
    - pip install git+https://github.com/huggingface/diffusers
    - requiremnts.txt: install all the requirements
    - accelerate config default
    - have hf write token
        module load conda/latest

        conda activate Diffusion 

    - train_dreambooth.py: training procedure for UNET and text encoder
    
          accelerate launch Diffusers/train_dreambooth.py \
          --pretrained_model_name_or_path="CompVis/stable-diffusion-v1-4" \
          --instance_data_dir="VISA/candle" \
          --output_dir="results/VISA/candle" \
          --instance_prompt="a photo of sks anomalous candle" \
          --resolution=512 \
          --train_batch_size=1 \
          --gradient_accumulation_steps=1 \
          --learning_rate=5e-6 \
          --lr_scheduler="constant" \
          --lr_warmup_steps=0 \
          --max_train_steps=400 \
          --push_to_hub
          
          
          
          # prior preservation loss
          
          accelerate launch Diffusers/train_dreambooth.py \ 
          --pretrained_model_name_or_path="CompVis/stable-diffusion-v1-4"  \
          --instance_data_dir="VISA/custom/anomaly" \
          --class_data_dir="VISA/custom/normal"\
          --output_dir="results/VISA/custom_cond"  \
          --with_prior_preservation --prior_loss_weight=1.0 \
          --instance_prompt="a photo of sks candle, a photo of ibc capsule, a photo of ucn cashew" \
          --class_prompt="a photo of candle, a photo of capsule, a photo of cashew" \
          --resolution=512 \
          --train_batch_size=1 \
          --gradient_accumulation_steps=1 \
          --learning_rate=5e-6 \
          --lr_scheduler="constant" \
          --lr_warmup_steps=0 \
          --num_class_images=18 \
          --max_train_steps=400 \
          --push_to_hub
